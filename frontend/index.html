Obtaining Google Credentials

Follow these 6 simple steps to generate your Gmail OAuth credentials:

  1. Open the Google Cloud Console.
  2. Create a new project (e.g. SCP-Automation).
  3. Go to APIs & Services > Library and search for Gmail API, then click Enable.
  4. Go to OAuth consent screen, select External, add your email (koushalcharn22@gmail.com) as a Test User.
  5. Go to Credentials > Create Credentials > OAuth client ID, choose Desktop App.
  6. Download the file, rename it to credentials.json, and upload it in this dashboard!
