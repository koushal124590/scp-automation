1. Overview & Purpose

SCP Automation ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an intelligent email workflow automation platform designed to help professionals manage and automate auto-replies, business card attachments, and follow-ups through Gmail.

We take user privacy extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we access, use, store, and protect your information when you connect your Google Account to SCP Automation.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

SCP Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No Human Viewing: We do not allow humans to read your email messages unless you have given affirmative agreement for specific messages, or it is necessary for security purposes.
  • No Advertising: User data obtained through Google Workspace APIs is NEVER used for serving advertisements, retargeting, or profiling.
  • No Data Brokering: We NEVER sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or email data to any third party.

3. Information We Access and How We Use It

When you choose to authenticate with Google, SCP Automation requests the following specific permissions:

  • Email Address & Profile Info (userinfo.email): Used exclusively to identify your authenticated session and display your active automation status.
  • Gmail Access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify): Used strictly to detect unread inquiries and dispatch your configured automated response containing your personalized business card and follow-up message.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security protocols to safeguard all authorization credentials:

  • OAuth 2.0 access tokens are encrypted and transmitted exclusively over secure HTTPS channels.
  • Your email content is processed in-memory for automated response evaluation and is not permanently indexed or stored on our databases.
  • You retain complete control over your account and may revoke SCP Automation's access at any time via your Google Security Permissions.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your session data or disconnect your account at any time. Upon disconnecting, all cached session tokens are purged immediately.
